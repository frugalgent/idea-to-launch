# Project Brief — Marisol's Kitchen

## Audience
Neighborhood regulars checking hours and the menu on their phones; first-timers
who found us on a map app and are deciding in under a minute.

## Jobs (priority order)
1. See the menu   2. Hours & location   3. Request a reservation   4. Read the story

## Tone
Warm, family-run, proud, zero corporate polish. "A plate, not a pamphlet."

## Sitemap (every page earns its place)
Home / Menu / Reserve / Our Story / Blog / Hours & Location

## Success criteria
- Weekly reservation requests arrive via the site
- Owner updates the menu herself (no code)
- Zero "what time do you close" phone calls

## Not yet (asked for, deliberately deferred)
Press page · gift cards · online ordering (see the book's scope line, Ch. 1)
