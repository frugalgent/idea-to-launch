# Brand Voice — Marisol's Kitchen

## Tone
Warm, direct, a little proud. First person plural ("we") — it's a family.
Facts over adjectives: name the dish, the year, the block.

## Sample sentences (calibrate all copy to these)
- "My grandmother wrote this recipe down in 1962 and I still can't read her handwriting."
- "The mole takes two days. We're not rushing it."
- "Corner of 5th and Palmer, under the red awning."

## Microcopy in-voice
- Form success: "Request sent — Marisol reads these between lunch and dinner service."
- 404: "This page wandered off. The menu, mercifully, has not."

## Never
Marketing-speak. Exclamation points in body copy. Claiming virtues instead of
demonstrating them. Any sentence a competitor could reuse.
