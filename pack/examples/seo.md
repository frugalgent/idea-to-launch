# SEO Rules — Marisol's Kitchen

## Per-page requirements (no page ships without)
- Unique <title> ≤ 60 chars, front-loaded ("Menu — Marisol's Kitchen")
- Meta description ≤ 155 chars, in brand voice, with one concrete fact
- One h1; headings in order; canonical URL (apex, not www — Ch. 12)
- Internal links use real anchor text, never "click here"

## Technical baseline (site-wide, verified at launch)
sitemap.xml · robots.txt · clean URLs · redirects for anything moved ·
images optimized + lazy-loaded (Ch. 6/9 — speed is an SEO issue)

## Structured data (written by Claude Code, validated free)
- LocalBusiness (name, address, hours, geo) on every page footer
- Article on blog posts · FAQ where genuine questions exist · Event for specials

## Local
- Google Business Profile claimed; NAP (name/address/phone) IDENTICAL everywhere
- "Corner of 5th and Palmer" phrasing consistent across site, GBP, and directories

## Content rule
Thin AI filler hurts rankings — anti-slop.md is also an SEO document.
Search Console connected; check monthly (maintenance calendar).
