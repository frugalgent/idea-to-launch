# Privacy & Legal Rules — Marisol's Kitchen

## Data inventory (every place visitor data lands)
| Data | Collected where | Lands where | Kept how long |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reservation requests (name, contact, date, party) | Reserve form | Airtable Requests table | 90 days after visit, then purged |
| Analytics | Cloudflare Web Analytics | Cloudflare (cookieless, no personal profiles) | Per Cloudflare retention |

## Collection rules
- Collect the MINIMUM: every form field is a promise made to a stranger
- No fields "for marketing." No birthdays. No addresses.
- New feature check: does it touch personal data? → update this inventory FIRST

## Retention hygiene
- Quarterly purge of Requests older than 90 days (on the maintenance calendar)
- Deletion requests honored within 30 days; the inventory above is the checklist

## Trust pages (live before launch)
Privacy policy · Terms of service (incl. no-show/cancellation language) · Accessibility statement
Drafted with Claude from THIS inventory; reviewed by a human; dated.

## The line
Claude drafts, templates guide — none of it is legal advice. Payments, health data,
minors' data, or scale = hire a lawyer. (Appendix D has the when-to-hire guide.)
